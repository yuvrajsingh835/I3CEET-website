Peer Review Process

Ensuring Excellence and Integrity in Scientific Research

EVALUATION WORKFLOW

1

Submission

Authors submit research via Microsoft CMT. Papers are initially screened for technical relevance and scope.

2

Technical Check

Preliminary check for formatting, language clarity, and adherence to submission guidelines.

3

Reviewer Allocation

Each paper is assigned to at least two independent expert reviewers to avoid conflicts of interest.

4

Evaluation

Reviewers assess significance, technical accuracy, novelty, and practical impact of the work.

5

Decision

TPC makes a final call based on reviewer feedback: Accept, Minor/Major Revision, or Reject.

6

Final Steps

Authors submit camera-ready versions. Final inclusion depends on registration and presentation.

Ethics Statement

The conference is committed to the highest standards of academic integrity. All submitted papers must be original and free from plagiarism. Violations involving data fabrication or unethical research will result in immediate rejection. We strictly follow a fair, confidential, and unbiased peer-review process.
