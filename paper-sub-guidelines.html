Originality & Scope

Manuscripts must be original, unpublished, and not under consideration elsewhere.
Significant and novel contributions relevant to conference themes are required.
Plagiarism Alert: Exceeding 20% similarity (iThenticate) may lead to immediate rejection.

Formatting Requirements

Maximum length: 5 pages (including figures and references).
Template: Must follow the official IEEE conference format.
Language: All manuscripts must be in professional English.
