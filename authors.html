Information for Authors

Guidelines and instructions for I3CEET-2027 presenters

Important Notice

If you are an author or presenter at I3CEET-2027, please adhere to these guidelines to ensure a high-quality academic experience.

  • All accepted and presented quality papers will be indexed by Scopus.
  • Acceptance of articles is made with the understanding that at least one author will attend the conference to deliver a presentation; severe disruption to a session is caused if an article is not presented.
  • To submit the final version, at least one author must register for the conference.
  • The nominal page limit for all articles is five (5) pages; articles exceeding these limits are subject to a maximum of 8 pages with an over-length fee.
  • Authors should bring their own notebooks/tablets/laptops for presentations. Projectors support HDMI / Type-C connectivity.
