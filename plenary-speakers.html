SPEAKERS

MR. SATISH KABADE

IEEE Senior Member | Expert in AI-driven Pension Systems

Satish Kabade is a pioneering leader in pension technology, known for transforming outdated pension infrastructures into agile, AI-driven systems. His work integrates artificial intelligence, machine learning, and cloud-native architectures to enhance efficiency, security, and transparency across the retirement sector. He has achieved measurable improvements, such as a 63% reduction in infrastructure costs and significant gains in fraud detection accuracy.

DR. KELVIN ANOH

Senior Lecturer, University of Chichester, United Kingdom

Dr Kelvin Anoh is a Senior Lecturer in Electrical and Electronic Engineering and Computing at the University of Chichester, UK. He is the Programme Leader of the Electrical and Electronic Engineering programme and Research Centre Head of the Centre for Future Technologies. His research focus includes Smart Infrastructure, Smart Energy Cities, Renewable Energy & Sustainability.

MR. RAKESH CHARLA

Senior Software Engineer, Microsoft Security

Rakesh Charla is a Senior Software Engineer within Microsoft Security, designs and delivers scalable, AI-driven security solutions that unify detection, investigation, and response across Microsoft 365 and Azure ecosystems. He plays a key role in driving Microsoft’s Zero Trust strategy by enabling faster detection and stronger threat response across enterprise systems.

MR. SATHISH KRISHNA ANUMULA

Digital Transformation Architect | Sr. IEEE Member

Sathish Krishna Anumula is a seasoned Digital Transformation Architect and Technology Strategist with 22+ years of experience advancing Closed Loop Manufacturing. His expertise spans enterprise architecture, hybrid cloud, IIoT, and AI/ML for industrial applications. He is a published author and a frequent keynote speaker in digital manufacturing innovation.

MR. AJIT KUMAR SAHU

Engineering Director, Data Safeguard Inc., California

Ajit Sahu is a senior Engineering Leader and AI Innovator with 16+ years of experience building high-performance engineering teams. As Director of Engineering at Data Safeguard Inc., he leads strategy for ID-PRIVACY®, the leading enterprise privacy platform powered by Responsible & Ethical AI. He is an IEEE Senior Member and Forbes Tech Council contributor.
