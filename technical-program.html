PROGRAM OVERVIEW

The I3CEET-2027 Technical Program will feature a comprehensive lineup of keynote talks, tutorials, and technical sessions. The full schedule will be released following the finalization of the peer-review process.

CONFERENCE TRACKS

Track 1- Communication, Networking and Broadcast Technologies
Wireless and Mobile Communication, 5G/6G Networks, Satellite and Space Communications, Broadcast Engineering, Mobile Ad-hoc Networks, Network Security and Broadcast Protocols.
Track 2- Components, Circuits, Devices and Systems
VLSI Design and Testing, Analog and Digital Circuits, Semiconductor Devices, Embedded Systems, Circuit Theory, Micro-Electro-Mechanical Systems (MEMS), and Electronic Component Modeling.
Track 3- Computing and Processing
Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Analytics, Big Data, High-Performance Computing, Cloud and Edge Computing, Computer Architecture, and Distributed Processing.
Track 4- Fields, Waves and Electromagnetics
Antenna Design and Wave Propagation, Microwave Engineering, Radar Systems, Electromagnetic Interference/Compatibility (EMI/EMC), and Advanced Meta-materials.
Track 5- Photonics and Electrooptics
Optical Fiber Communication, Laser Technology, Optoelectronics, Photonic Integrated Circuits, Biophotonics, and Quantum Optics.
Track 6- Power, Energy and Industry Applications
Smart Grids, Renewable Energy Systems, Electric Vehicles, Power Electronics, Industrial Automation, Control Systems, and Sustainable Energy Technologies.
