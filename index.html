WELCOME TO I3CEET-2027

The 2nd International Conference on Computing, Communication and Energy Efficiency Technologies (I3CEET-2027) will be held on 29–30 April 2027 at Galgotias College of Engineering and Technology (GCET), Greater Noida, India.

I3CEET-2027 provides a premier platform for researchers, academicians, and industry professionals to present and discuss recent advances in computing, communication, and energy efficiency technologies, fostering collaboration and innovation aligned with the conference themes.
